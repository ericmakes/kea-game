## 1. WHAT IT IS

A DOC swing-bridge crossing on a braided glacier river. Four things carry it:

- **THE SWING BRIDGE** — the signature structure, and the first thing in the tour that is a
  SPAN: two A-frame towers, a deck of slats, wire handrails, a 24 m crossing over the water.
  It is the map's climbable ridge the way the verandah is the village's, except it is high,
  narrow and over a river, which changes what falling off it means.
- **THE BOARDWALK** — a raised timber walkway from the carpark end to the bridge, because a
  braided riverbed is stones and DOC does not let you walk on them. Low, wide, and the map's
  organising line.
- **THE GLACIER LAKE WITH FLOES** — at the upstream end: milky blue water with pale ice floes
  sitting in it. The floes are the one genuinely new thing on this map — they FLOAT, which is
  the first surface in the game that moves under the bird.
- **TOURISTS MID-SELFIE** — the cast, and the joke. Three of them, phones up, backs to the
  bird, at the bridge mouth and the lake edge. Everything they own is on the ground.

Plus: shingle banks and braid channels, a DOC information shelter at the carpark end, a jetboat
drawn up on the shingle, a pile of daypacks, and beech along the far bank.

**THE WATER IS NOT A NEW MATERIAL PIECE.** A glacier river is milky pale blue-grey and almost
opaque — that is what the `nz_` plates show and it is why it is called rock flour. So it is a
vertex-coloured plane with the `snow` family's roughness behaviour, not a transmission shader.
If it wants more later that is its own piece; inventing a water material inside a map piece is
how the shopfront glass nearly went wrong (TODO 43).

## 2. THE MISSIONS — 8 TO 12, TWO STAR PAGES

`G.chapters=[A.bridge, A.lake]`.

**THE SWING BRIDGE** — hold the span, work the deck slats loose, take a selfie stick mid-shot,
raid the daypacks stacked at the bridge mouth, ride the bridge while it sways.

**THE GLACIER LAKE** — stand on a floe and let it carry you, rob the jetboat, take somebody's
lunch off the shingle, get the DOC shelter roof, drop something shiny in the water.

A **finale declared with the mission** — the far tower of the bridge, which is the highest thing
on the map and the one place you can only reach by crossing.

**NOT ONE MISSION ID ON ANOTHER MAP, IN EITHER MODE.** Five lists now.

## 3. THE MAP DECLARATIONS

- **CAST** — three tourists. Declared, not empty: the missions want somebody to steal from.

- **SNOW ENVELOPE** — none; a river flat in summer. Declared null, asserted zero.
- **ROAD LANES** — none. A riverbed has no road. Declared absent, asserted zero.
- **TRAVEL ANCHOR** — over the river looking down the crossing.
