it at `assets/models/kea_astra.glb`. Inspected before this brief was written:

| property | Astra's kea.glb | our kea_bill.glb |
|---|---|---|
| triangles | 20,000 (suspiciously round) | 4,927 |
| vertices | 11,082 | 2,987 |
| joints | **161 — the crest is BACK** | 101 (crest removed) |
| bone names | `kea_UpperMandible_bone_07` etc. | `UpperMandible` etc. |
| animation | `Animation_01`, 311 channels | same clip retargeted |
| textures | **4096x2048 painted baseColour + 4K feather normal** | source cockatoo maps |
| material | alpha-MASK, doubleSided, cutoff 0.45, rough 0.84 | MeshStandard |
| skin weights | clean — all rows sum 1.0, no orphans | as bound in P5b |
| UVs | fill 0..1 fully, authored for ITS layout | inherited from source |
| generator | "Kea reference adaptation / GLB 2.0" — not Blender | Blender 5.2.1 |

**The prize is the texture, not the mesh.** Its baseColour contains what our
pipeline explicitly could not synthesise (TODO 84): real feather SCALLOPING —
dark-rimmed feathers across the body — plus painted green wing coverts, a slate
bill with a nostril, and a full orange underwing panel with barring.

**Three defects as delivered:** every bone renamed (our rig binds by exact
name, so it will not drive); the 60-joint cockatoo crest is back (built from
`rockatoo.glb`, not our de-crested base — Eric's explicit call undone); and the
geometry appears to be a NEW mesh wearing the old skeleton — a wings-spread
layout of alpha-card feathers — not an edit of ours.

## 1. PATH A — HARVEST THE TEXTURE ONTO OUR MESH  (try this first)

Our mesh is correct: long kea bill (P5e), no crest, our bone names, a proven
binding, 4,927 tris. Astra's paint is the missing layer. If its UV layout is
compatible with ours, this is a straight material swap and we get both.

**Test compatibility BEFORE any transplant, and by measurement:** both files
descend from `rockatoo.glb`, so the UVs may or may not survive. Compare our
mesh's TEXCOORD_0 against Astra's per-region. If they disagree, do NOT force
it — a mismatched atlas paints a bill onto a wing.

If compatible: apply `Kea_BaseColour` + `Kea_Feather_Normal` to our mesh, with
Astra's material settings (alphaMode MASK cutoff 0.45, doubleSided, roughness
0.84, normalScale 0.6) as the starting point, then judge.

If NOT compatible, the fallback is a **bake**: transfer Astra's surface colour
onto our UV layout in Blender (bake from its mesh to ours by proximity). That
is a real job but a known one, and it keeps our geometry.

## 2. PATH B — EVALUATE ITS MESH AS A REPLACEMENT BASE  (only if A fails)

If the texture cannot be moved, the question becomes whether to adopt Astra's
model wholesale. It is NOT a drop-in. To evaluate it fairly:

1. **Rename map.** Its names are systematically transformed: `kea_` prefix,
   `_bone_NN` suffix. Strip both and the original names fall out. Build the map
   programmatically, assert every one of our required rig names resolves, and
   fail loudly on any that does not.
2. **Remove the crest again** — 60 `FeatherHead` joints and their geometry, the
   same operation P5d performed and for the same reason Eric gave: a kea has a
   smooth rounded crown.
3. **Bind and pose it.** The open question no inspection can answer: its
   silhouette is wings-fully-spread with feathers as individual alpha cards.
   Does it FOLD correctly when our rig closes the wing, or do the cards fan and
   intersect? Shoot it folded and open. This decides Path B outright.
4. **Cost check.** 20,000 tris and 14 MB of texture against our 4,927 and much
   less. Measure the frame budget at the Retina framebuffer as P4 did; report
   it rather than assume it is fine.
5. Verify the seal mission completes 12/12 headless and every mission anchor
   resolves on it.

## 3. WHAT GETS DECIDED BY ERIC, NOT BY THE GATE

Shoot the four judging angles (head, posture, folded, wings-open) for whichever
path succeeds, alongside the same angles from our current `kea_bill.glb`, as a
side-by-side strip. Eric picks. Do not adopt Astra's model on measurement
alone — its texture may be better while its geometry is worse, and the strip is
how that gets seen.

## 4. LICENCE — this is not optional

Astra's file is a derivative of `rockatoo.glb` (CC-BY 4.0, Macauley.B). It gets
its own ledger row in `assets/LICENCES.md` with its own `<!-- ASSET -->` marker,
naming the derivation chain, what changed, its md5, and the fact that the
textures were machine-generated by GPT-6 Astra from the reference plates. The
credits screen requirement is unchanged: CC-BY attribution must remain visible
in-game. A battery already cross-checks the ledger against the credits and the
bytes — it will go red until the row exists, which is correct.

**Also record honestly:** the provenance of machine-painted textures derived
from reference photographs is not a settled question. The plates are Eric's
collected reference; the paint is a model's output. Note it in the ledger row
rather than leaving it undocumented.
