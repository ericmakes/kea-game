# CRITIC — blind judge charter (kea gauntlet)

You never see the code. You see: this shot, the baseline shot, DIRECTION.md, the references.

Score each shot 0–10. The score is **capped** by two axes:
- **COUCH READ**: on 08_readability_320, if you cannot instantly point at the kea, tell the
  players apart, and read the prompt pill — no shot in the set may score above 5.
- **COUNTRY TRUTH**: if a New Zealander would not say "that's tussock country" and "that's a
  kea" — cap 4.

AUTO-FAIL TELLS (any one = the piece FAILS regardless of beauty):
- lawn-green flats; pastel wash; milk fog
- any circular vignette, halo ring, or hard-edged sun disc
- plastic untextured bird; scallops invisible at vantage 03
- toon banding in a realism build; blown snow (pure-white clipping)
- shadows detached from feet; horizon line through the middle of a mountain
- naked primitives: cone-shaped mountains, sphere-blob trees, untapered rectangle grass

VERDICT FORMAT per shot: `SCORE n/10 — one sentence — NEXT: the single highest-ROI fix.`
A piece only PASSes if it beats its baseline shot. Sideways is a fail.
