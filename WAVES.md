# WAVES.md - the art-wave operating manual (supervised sessions)
Counterpart to OVERNIGHT.md. OVERNIGHT is the blind night shift; WAVES is
the daytime studio: Eric is PRESENT and JUDGING. Nothing in a wave ships
on the machine's taste.

## THE RITUAL (every wave, in order)
1. Certify the tip first: gate.sh prints CERTIFIED-SHIP and the md5
   matches the log. Never build on an assumed foundation.
2. Read ARTBIBLE.md and gauntlet/reference/board/BOARD.md. The wave brief
   names its judging pairs.
3. Before-strip: run node gauntlet/verify/sidebyside.mjs for the wave's
   vantages BEFORE changing anything.
4. VARIANT STRIPS - the law of best: for every taste call build 2-3
   parameterisations, reshoot the SAME judging vantage per variant,
   present the strip plus its sidebyside pair. Eric picks. Never ship
   attempt one unseen.
5. Lock the pick as NAMED CONSTANTS, then write assertions freezing the
   recipe - taste becomes law the night shift can protect. Append the
   locked recipe to ARTBIBLE.md.
6. House laws unchanged: fastgate every patch, battery proof for
   mechanical change, CERTIFIED-SHIP before any ship, one wave per
   session.
7. End of wave: reshoot ALL vantages (working-set freshness law), Eric
   judges flags, full re-pin sweep, commit "WAVE n: name - certified md5".
   Run the stability sweep LAST if anything staging-adjacent moved.
8. Rig anchors are sacred: mission grab points, HEADLESS guards, and
   existing battery contracts stay green. If a contract is genuinely
   unsatisfiable after a legitimate change, recalibrate with evidence per
   the session-3 pattern - never weaken silently.

## WAVE 3 - THE BIRD (pulled forward at Eric's call)
GOAL: the rig stops reading as blocks taped together and reads as the
bird on the wall. Stylized, not photoreal - UGG's goose is the fidelity
model: simple, unmistakable.
THE WORK (each item = its own variant strip and judgement):
- EYES: about 40 percent smaller, side-mounted, dark iris with a thin
  cream ring. Strip: 3 size/placement variants. Judge at 03 and 18
  against kea_head_01/02.
- BEAK: replace the stacked cones with ONE continuous lofted hook - deep
  at the base, smooth taper to the tip. Strip: 2 loft profiles. Judge at
  03 and 18 against kea_head_02.
- WING SEAT: tuck the folded-wing shell into the torso; kill the floating
  shelf. Judge at 03, 13, 25 against kea_posture_01/02.
- FEET: real zygodactyl toes (two forward, two back) that visibly grip on
  perch and hang. Judge at 13, 12, 25 against kea_feet_01.
- UNDERWING: scarlet to true coverage plus yellow/black barring on the
  flight feathers (procedural canvas fine, HEADLESS-guarded). Judge at 04
  against kea_underwing_01/02.
- SCALE: colossal to about 2.5x car roof height - one constant. Judge at
  09 against kea_scale_01 for proportion truth.
CONSTRAINTS: one file, r128, no new deps; the scallop feathering is KEPT
(it already earns its place); every mission anchor keeps working (seal
mission 12/12 in battery); the preen contract (PREEN.*) is recalibrated
with evidence if the new head or wing geometry moves its reference lines.
LOCK: a single KEA_RIG constants block; assertions freeze eye scale and
offset, beak profile dims, wing seat clearance, toe counts, and scale.
EXIT TEST: someone who knows the bird says "that's a kea" unprompted.
